### ✨ Core Capabilities

KeNote is built for speed, keyboard-first navigation, and zero friction:

- **Local-First .md Storage**: Your notes are plain `.md` files on your disk. No cloud lock-in, no telemetry.
- **Fast WYSIWYG Markdown**: Real-time rendering for headers, bold, italics, strikethrough, blockquotes, and lists.
- **Always-on-Top Pinning**: Keep critical checklists floating above IDEs and browsers with `Ctrl + P`.
- **Keyboard-First Navigation**: Access everything without lifting your fingers from the keyboard.
